# Suggested battle tactics

These are recommended starting configurations for the player's unit stacks,
based on each encounter's enemy roster and the strengths of the available
formations and orders. They are tactical guidance, not a guaranteed global
optimum: the best setup can change with army size, research, upgrades, and which
units the player owns.

Set formations and orders per unit type in the Army tab. Units not called out
below can stay on Standard. `Focus Strong` targets the largest reachable enemy
stack; `Focus Counter` only gets a special target preference when that unit has
a matching `bonusVs` tag. Use `Focus Weak` when quickly removing a small,
dangerous stack is more useful than concentrating fire on a large one.

Availability matters: Age 0–1 have no active formations or special orders.
Orders other than Standard unlock at Age 3. Rush and Turtle require their
research; Skirmish requires `raiding_vessels`; Pike Square and Shield Wall
require `tercio_formations`. If a suggested formation is still locked, use
Frontline for a screen or Backline for ranged damage.

## Age 0 — Ancient

These battles have no formation or order choices; leave both at Standard.

### First Fire — `age0_1_first_fire`

- **Formation:** Standard.
- **Order:** Standard.
- **Plan:** Build a solid Tribesman/Warrior line; this is a basic strength check,
  not a matchup that rewards a specialized formation.

### Wolf Pack — `age0_2_wolf_pack`

- **Formation:** Standard.
- **Order:** Standard.
- **Plan:** Bring a durable infantry line for the wolves and enough ranged
  support to remove the bear without trading the whole front line.

### Bronze Raid — `age0_3_bronze_raid`

- **Formation:** Standard.
- **Order:** Standard.
- **Plan:** Mix Bronze Spearmen against the Axemen with ranged units against the
  Slingers; avoid relying on only one damage role.

### Fall of Troy — `age0_boss_troy`

- **Formation:** Standard.
- **Order:** Standard.
- **Plan:** Use Bronze Spearmen against the Chariots, Bronze Bowmen against the
  ground line, and Triremes to contest the enemy ship.

## Age 1 — Iron

No formation or special-order choices are active yet; leave both at Standard.

### Kadesh — `age1_1_kadesh_iron`

- **Formation:** Standard.
- **Order:** Standard.
- **Plan:** Use Iron Spearmen to hold the Chariot charge, with Iron/Bronze
  ranged units to thin the infantry and bowmen.

### Assyrian Siege — `age1_2_assyrian_siege`

- **Formation:** Standard.
- **Order:** Standard.
- **Plan:** Prioritize ranged siege counters for the Ballista and bring a
  Quinquereme or other naval force to answer the War Galleys.

### The Sea Peoples — `age1_boss_iron_collapse`

- **Formation:** Standard.
- **Order:** Standard.
- **Plan:** Field a broad counter-army: Iron Spearmen and Elephants for the
  armored ground force, ranged troops for the bowmen, and Quinqueremes for the
  fleet.

## Age 2 — Classical

Special orders are not available until Age 3, so use Standard order throughout.
Frontline/Backline are the basic formations; Rush and Turtle are useful when
their research is available.

### Marathon — `age2_1_marathon`

- **Formation:** Hoplites/Legionaries Frontline; Archers Backline; keep naval
  units Standard or Frontline.
- **Order:** Standard.
- **Plan:** Protect the ranged line while it removes the enemy archers; use
  naval units to handle the Trader rather than leaving it uncontested.

### Cannae — `age2_2_cannae`

- **Formation:** Legionaries Frontline or Turtle; cavalry/Companions Rush;
  Archers and Onagers Backline; Fire Ships Standard.
- **Order:** Standard.
- **Plan:** The massed enemy archers are the main threat. Turtle the infantry
  screen if unlocked, and use ranged units and Onagers to reduce that stack
  while the cavalry pressures its supports.

### Gaugamela — `age2_boss_gaugamela`

- **Formation:** Legionaries/Hoplites Turtle or Frontline; cavalry Rush;
  Archers and Onagers Backline; naval units Standard.
- **Order:** Standard.
- **Plan:** Hold the cavalry and elephant charge with a durable front; keep
  ranged damage safe long enough to break the large enemy infantry stacks.

## Age 3 — Middle Ages

Special orders unlock at this age. Use Focus Strong against a dominant stack,
and Focus Counter only on units whose `bonusVs` matches an enemy tag.

### Battle of Tours — `age3_1_tours`

- **Formation:** Man-at-Arms Turtle; Longbows/Crossbows Backline or Skirmish;
  Knights Rush; siege units Backline.
- **Order:** Man-at-Arms Focus Strong; Crossbows Focus Counter where their
  counter applies; ranged units Focus Strong to concentrate on the large
  Longbow/Knight stacks.
- **Plan:** A Turtle Man-at-Arms line with Focus Strong is the recommended
  anchor; it wins against this encounter in the supplied Age 3 save.

### Hastings — `age3_2_hastings`

- **Formation:** Longbows/Crossbows Skirmish; Man-at-Arms Turtle or Frontline;
  Knights Rush; siege units Backline.
- **Order:** Ranged units Focus Strong to hit the oversized Longbow stack;
  cavalry Focus Strong; counter units Focus Counter when applicable.
- **Plan:** This is the Skirmish showcase: protect your ranged force from the
  enemy's much larger Longbow stack while your front line buys it time.

### Castillon — `age3_boss_castillon`

- **Formation:** Man-at-Arms Turtle/Frontline; Longbows and Crossbows Skirmish
  or Backline; Trebuchets/Bombards Backline; Knights Rush; Caravels/Longships
  Frontline or Rush.
- **Order:** Crossbows and counter-capable siege Focus Counter; other ranged
  units Focus Strong; cavalry and naval units Focus Strong.
- **Plan:** Bring a genuinely mixed roster. Keep siege and ranged stacks
  protected while armored ground and naval units contest the enemy's diverse
  line.

## Age 4 — Renaissance

Pike Square is especially effective against cavalry. Shield Wall is a sturdy
screen against cavalry and ranged fire.

### Pavia — `age4_1_pavia`

- **Formation:** Tercio Pikemen Pike Square; other infantry Shield Wall;
  Musketeers/Cannons Backline; Dragoons Skirmish; Cuirassiers Rush; Galleons
  Backline.
- **Order:** Pikemen Focus Strong against the heavy cavalry; counter-capable
  units Focus Counter; ranged units Focus Strong.
- **Plan:** Hold the cavalry charge with pikes and shielded infantry, then let
  the ranged line remove the enemy's cavalry and shot stacks.

### Lepanto — `age4_2_lepanto`

- **Formation:** War Galleys Frontline; Frigates Skirmish; Galleons and
  Cannons Backline; Privateers/Caravels Rush; Longships Frontline.
- **Order:** Cannons and other counter-capable units Focus Counter; remaining
  ranged/naval stacks Focus Strong.
- **Plan:** Keep durable ships in front of the gun line, with fast raiders
  pressing the enemy's rear. Avoid sending every ship into the same role.

### Vienna 1683 — `age4_boss_vienna`

- **Formation:** Tercio Pikemen Pike Square; Man-at-Arms Shield Wall;
  Musketeers/Cannons Backline; Dragoons Skirmish; Cuirassiers Rush; Galleons
  Backline.
- **Order:** Pikemen Focus Strong against cavalry; counter-capable units Focus
  Counter; ranged units Focus Strong.
- **Plan:** This is the strongest pike-and-shot matchup: pikes blunt cavalry,
  Shield Wall screens the shooters, and artillery/ranged stacks focus down
  threats rather than splitting fire.

## Age 5 — Industrial

Use the Age 4 formations where their research is available. Skirmish works well
on mobile ranged units and aircraft; keep artillery in Backline.

### Waterloo — `age5_1_waterloo`

- **Formation:** Line Infantry Shield Wall; Lancers/Cuirassiers Rush; Dragoons
  Skirmish; Howitzers Backline; any Tercio Pikemen Pike Square.
- **Order:** Pike Square units Focus Strong against cavalry; counter-capable
  units Focus Counter; artillery Focus Strong.
- **Plan:** Protect artillery with an infantry line, use pikes to meet cavalry,
  and send mobile cavalry around the front instead of forming one uniform
  charge.

### Gettysburg — `age5_2_gettysburg`

- **Formation:** Line Infantry and Ironclads Frontline/Shield Wall; Riflemen,
  Gatlings, and Howitzers Backline; Dragoons Skirmish; Lancers Rush.
- **Order:** Gatlings/Howitzers Focus Strong on the largest enemy stacks;
  counter-capable units Focus Counter; mobile cavalry Focus Weak to finish
  damaged supports.
- **Plan:** Keep the gun line behind a durable screen and use the mobile units
  to pressure the enemy artillery and ranged line.

### The Somme — `age5_boss_somme`

- **Formation:** Line Infantry Shield Wall; Riflemen/Gatlings/Howitzers
  Backline; Biplanes and Zeppelins Skirmish; Submarines Standard or Backline;
  Ironclads Frontline.
- **Order:** Howitzers and other counter-capable units Focus Counter against
  armor/naval targets; aircraft and ranged stacks Focus Strong.
- **Plan:** Preserve the artillery and aircraft: let infantry and Ironclads
  screen while mobile air and naval stacks attack the enemy's exposed ranged
  force.

## Age 6 — Modern / Space

Use Skirmish for mobile air/ranged units, Rush for assault units, and
Backline for long-range artillery. Mix surface, air, and ground units to avoid
leaving a domain uncontested.

### Midway — `age6_1_midway`

- **Formation:** Battleships and Carriers Frontline/Backline respectively;
  Fighters Skirmish; Bombers Backline; Submarines Backline or Skirmish;
  Infantry Frontline.
- **Order:** Bombers and anti-armor units Focus Counter where applicable;
  Fighters Focus Strong against the largest air/naval threat; surface ships
  Focus Strong.
- **Plan:** Protect the carrier and bomber line with surface ships; use fighters
  to contest enemy air and submarines to pressure the fleet.

### Berlin 1945 — `age6_2_berlin_1945`

- **Formation:** Infantry Shield Wall; Tanks Rush; Mechanized Dragoons
  Skirmish; Fighters Skirmish; Bombers/Howitzers Backline.
- **Order:** Anti-armor units Focus Counter against tanks; artillery and
  aircraft Focus Strong; infantry Focus Strong against the largest ground
  stack.
- **Plan:** Break the massed infantry with ranged fire while tanks and
  mechanized units pressure the enemy's artillery and air support.

### Desert Storm — `age6_3_desert_storm`

- **Formation:** Soldiers Shield Wall; Abrams Rush; Jets and Helicopters
  Skirmish; Artillery Backline; Destroyers Frontline; Nuclear Submarines
  Backline.
- **Order:** Anti-armor units Focus Counter against tanks; aircraft Focus
  Strong against the largest reachable threat; naval units Focus Strong.
- **Plan:** Coordinate armor and air rather than relying on tanks alone; keep
  artillery protected and use the fleet to contest naval targets.

### Drone War — `age6_4_drone_war`

- **Formation:** Special Forces Shield Wall/Frontline; Abrams Rush; Drones and
  Fighters Skirmish; Artillery/Stealth Bombers Backline; Destroyers Frontline;
  Nuclear Submarines Backline.
- **Order:** Anti-armor units Focus Counter against tanks; ranged/air stacks
  Focus Strong against the largest drone or artillery stack.
- **Plan:** This is a layered air-and-ground fight: preserve the artillery,
  counter the drone line with air, and use armor to pressure its support.

### Orbital War — `age6_boss_orbital`

- **Formation:** Mech Suits Shield Wall; Starships Rush; Carriers Frontline;
  Orbital Lasers/Satellites/Stealth Bombers Backline; Fighters Skirmish.
- **Order:** Anti-armor units Focus Counter against Mechs/Starships where
  applicable; orbital and ranged stacks Focus Strong to concentrate fire on
  the largest reachable stack.
- **Plan:** Keep the orbital damage dealers alive behind a durable screen and
  use Starships/Fighters to disrupt the enemy's orbital back line.
